Through flagship initiatives such as the Education Innovation Cafe (EIC), Hart's Innovation Playground, Mobile Maker-Lab, Robotics for Social Change Competition, we provide hands-on training, mentorship, and opportunities in fields like artificial intelligence, robotics, data science, and sustainable innovation.

Empowering youth and Communities through Digital Literacy, Education, Innovation and Global Advocacy

HART'S Haiti Inc. is a nonprofit organization pioneering education, creativity, and technology for social impact. We equip youth from vulnerable communities with hands-on experience in robotics, AI, data, and digital innovation - bridging creativity with technology to solve Haiti's most pressing challenges.

Our Programs

Education Innov. Cafe

The Education Innovation Cafe (EIC) is a creative and transformative hub designed to bridge education, technology, and community development. Through a welcoming, hybrid learning hub that brings together community, technology, and creativity - over coffee - we aim to empower and equip a new generation of change-makers.

Learn more

Innovation Playground

Hart's Innovation Playground is a selective, high-level technology community designed for young innovators (ages 18-35) with mid-level technical experience, seeking to advance their skills into specialist-level expertise. We provide a structured pathway through hands-on sessions using robotics, prototyping, IoT, and advanced engineering kits.

Learn more

Digital Literacy

The Digital Literacy program equips youth and underserved communities with essential 21st-century skills to thrive in a tech-driven world. Through engaging, hands-on sessions, participants learn how to safely and effectively use digital tools for learning, communication, job readiness, and social impact. Building digital empowerment across all levels.

Learn More

Mobile Maker-Lab

The Mobile Maker Lab is an innovative, traveling educational program designed to introduce Haitian students (ages 12-18) to robotics, prototyping, and hands-on creativity. Through a "touring" approach, the Lab moves between schools across different cities, bringing STEM opportunities directly to classrooms.

Learn more

With a vision of "building futures through creativity and technology", HART'S Haiti Inc. is shaping a movement where young people are not passive learners, but active innovators and changemakers, leaving a lasting legacy for Haiti and the world.
